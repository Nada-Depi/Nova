Project Overview

NOVA is a personal care and skincare brand offering simple and effective products designed to support everyday self-care for both men and women.

This project focuses on developing a digital marketing strategy for NOVA by understanding its target audience, analyzing competitors, creating relevant content, and building a strong digital presence. The project will demonstrate how effective content and digital marketing techniques can help NOVA connect with its audience, increase brand awareness, and build long-term customer engagement.

---

Project Objectives

- Build and strengthen NOVA's brand awareness across digital platforms.
- Identify and understand the brand's target audience and customer needs.
- Develop a clear and effective digital marketing strategy for the brand.
- Create engaging and relevant social media content that reflects NOVA's identity.
- Increase audience engagement and build trust and brand connection.
- Define suitable KPIs to measure the performance of the digital marketing strategy.

---

Project Scope

The project will cover:

- Brand Analysis — Understanding NOVA's identity, products, and positioning.
- Target Audience Analysis — Identifying the ideal audience and creating buyer personas.
- Competitor Analysis — Studying competitors and identifying opportunities for differentiation.
- Digital Marketing Strategy — Developing a suitable strategy based on the brand and audience.
- Content Strategy — Defining content pillars, content types, and communication style.
- Social Media Plan — Developing content ideas and a structured content calendar.
- Performance Measurement — Defining KPIs to evaluate the effectiveness of the strategy.

«Final Deliverable: A complete digital marketing strategy for NOVA, including brand and audience analysis, competitor analysis, content strategy, social media plan, sample content, KPIs, and final recommendations.»

---

Project Plan (5 Weeks)

Week 1 — Research & Brand Analysis

- Analyze NOVA's brand identity, products, and current positioning.
- Research the personal care and skincare market.
- Identify the target audience.
- Create buyer personas.
- Conduct competitor analysis.

Week 2 — Digital Marketing Strategy

- Define NOVA's digital marketing objectives.
- Develop the brand's positioning and key messaging.
- Select the most relevant digital platforms.
- Define the communication tone and style.
- Establish the main KPIs.

Week 3 — Content Strategy

- Define NOVA's content pillars.
- Develop content ideas suitable for the target audience.
- Create a social media content plan.
- Develop sample posts and creative concepts.
- Explore the use of AI tools in content creation and development.

Week 4 — Content Creation & Optimization

- Create and refine selected content samples.
- Review content consistency with NOVA's brand identity.
- Develop engagement-focused content ideas.
- Analyze the expected performance of the proposed content.
- Optimize the strategy based on findings.

Week 5 — Finalization & Presentation

- Review and finalize all project components.
- Complete the final digital marketing strategy.
- Organize project files and documentation.
- Prepare the final presentation.
- Present the final strategy, results, and recommendations.
